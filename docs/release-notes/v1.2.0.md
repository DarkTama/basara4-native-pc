## Sengoku Basara 4 PC v1.2.0 Highlights

### Dual-Monitor Co-op (EXPERIMENTAL)
- **Two-Screen Battle**: Play 2-player split-screen across two physical monitors using a custom 1920x2160 tall canvas. Player 1 is displayed full-screen on the left monitor and Player 2 on the right monitor.
- **F8 Mirror**: F8 toggles mirror mode (full frame on both monitors) for menus and cutscenes. Menus and cutscenes still look squashed and fullscreen banners can be cut at the monitor seam ([Issue #1](https://github.com/DarkTama/basara4-native-pc/issues/1)).
- **Experimental Notice**: Dual-monitor rendering incurs higher GPU workload. Known presentation and aspect ratio issues are actively tracked in [Issue #1](https://github.com/DarkTama/basara4-native-pc/issues/1).

### Launcher Redesign & Themes
- **Yoshiteru & Hisahide Themes**: Brand-new visual design with two selectable themes: Ashikaga Yoshiteru (violet & gold) and Matsunaga Hisahide (steel blue & amber). Switch between themes live in **Tools > Appearance**.
- **Modern UI Elements**: Card-based settings rows, custom toggle switches, and box cover art in the launcher navigation dock.

### Gamepad & Keyboard Launcher Navigation
- Full controller navigation (D-pad / left stick to navigate, A / Cross to activate, B / Circle to back) and keyboard arrow navigation with dynamic on-screen button prompts.

### "Report a Problem" & Crash Diagnostics
- Easily submit bug reports and feature requests directly from the left-dock button and from **Tools > Report a problem**.
- Optional crash reporting prompt after unexpected exits to help diagnose game issues.
- **Privacy-first**: All reports are anonymous, personal paths (usernames, home directories) and sensitive tokens are automatically scrubbed, raw minidumps are never uploaded, and you can inspect the exact payload with **Preview** before sending. Standard GitHub Issues remain fully supported.
